Comprehensive Diabetes Care Protocol

Document CP-DM-2025.1 · Effective measurement year 2025 · SYNTHETIC / DEMO guidance for the Care Protocol Context & Ontology project. HEDIS-aligned, not NCQA-certified. Not for clinical use.

1. Scope and Eligible Population

This protocol governs quality measurement and care escalation for adults with diabetes mellitus, identified by an ICD-10-CM diagnosis in the E10 (type 1), E11 (type 2), or E13 (other specified) families, including all descendant codes (e.g. E11.21 Type 2 diabetes with diabetic nephropathy, E11.319 … with unspecified diabetic retinopathy). Membership is by the diagnosis hierarchy, not an enumerated code list.

3. Glycemic Management

CP-GLY-1. Target HbA1c is < 7.0% for most adults. A1c between 8.0% and 9.0% warrants therapy review; A1c > 9.0% is classified poorly controlled and triggers escalation within 3 months.
CP-GLY-2. Metformin is first-line glucose-lowering therapy. Metformin is contraindicated when eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²; use with caution and do not initiate at eGFR 30–45.
CP-GLY-3. For patients with established CKD (eGFR ≥ 20) or heart failure, add an SGLT2 inhibitor regardless of A1c, for organ protection.

6. Care-Gap Escalation Logic

CP-ESC-1 (statin gap). A patient aged 40–75 with diabetes and no statin dispensed in the year (SPD gap) should be started on a moderate-to-high intensity statin, after confirming no contraindication (pregnancy, active hepatic disease).
CP-ESC-2 (kidney gap). A patient missing eGFR or uACR (KED gap) should have the missing component ordered. If uACR ≥ 30 mg/g, start an ACE inhibitor or ARB (not both).
CP-ESC-3 (combined cardiorenal). A patient with diabetes and chronic kidney disease (N18.*) and heart failure (I50.*) is a cardiorenal priority: ensure statin, ACE/ARB, and an SGLT2 inhibitor (if eGFR ≥ 20), and monitor A1c every 3 months.

Escalation rules reference measures (GSD/KED/SPD), labs (eGFR, uACR, A1c), diagnosis families (E10/E11/E13, N18, I50) and drug classes — the relationships this protocol contributes to the knowledge graph.
