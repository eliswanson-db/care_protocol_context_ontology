# CSL-A Clinical Study Protocol, version 1.0

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT for the Clinical Study Context & Evidence Lab. CSL-101 is a fictional compound. This protocol is
illustrative only. It is not a real trial, not regulatory-grade, and not for clinical use.

## 1 Synopsis

CSL-A is a 72-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of once-weekly subcutaneous CSL-101 10 mg in
adults with obesity, or with overweight and at least one weight-related comorbidity. Participants are randomized
2:1 to CSL-101 10 mg once weekly or placebo once weekly. CSL-101 is a synthetic investigational glucagon-like
peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist.

## 3 Objectives and endpoints

### 3.1 Primary endpoint

The primary endpoint is the percent change in body weight from baseline to Week 24. The derivation (baseline,
visit window, record selection and handling of missing data) is defined in the Statistical Analysis Plan, section
9.4.1. Body weight is collected as the CDISC biomedical concept Body Weight (C81328) and tabulated in the SDTM VS
domain with VSTESTCD = WEIGHT.

## 4 Study design

The study has a screening period of up to 4 weeks, a 72-week treatment period and a follow-up visit. The first dose
is given at the baseline visit (study day 1), after the baseline body weight is measured.

## 5 Study population

Participants must meet all inclusion criteria and none of the exclusion criteria at screening.

### 5.1 Inclusion criteria

1. Age 18 to 75 years at screening.
2. Body mass index (BMI) of 30 kg/m2 or more; or BMI of 27 kg/m2 or more with at least one weight-related
   comorbidity. BMI is calculated from the screening weight and height. If no screening weight is available, the
   earliest valid pre-dose weight is used.
3. Weight-related comorbidities are: hypertensive disorder, hyperlipidemia, hypertriglyceridemia, type 2 diabetes
   mellitus, prediabetes, coronary artery disease, heart failure, stroke, peripheral arterial disease and
   metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), including any more specific form of these
   conditions. Pulmonary hypertension, pregnancy-induced hypertension and congenital coronary artery malformations
   are not weight-related comorbidities for this criterion, even where they are classified under one of the listed
   conditions.

### 5.2 Exclusion criteria

1. History of medullary thyroid carcinoma or multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2, including familial forms.
2. History of acute or chronic pancreatitis.
3. Type 1 diabetes mellitus.
4. Diabetic retinopathy.
5. Bipolar disorder or schizophrenia.
6. Any condition that is a recorded contraindication for any member of the GLP-1 receptor agonist class. Known
   hypersensitivity is assessed by the investigator against CSL-101 itself. A history of allergy to an unrelated
   drug is not exclusionary.
7. Use of a prohibited medication (section 6) within 90 days before screening.

## 6 Prohibited medications

The following are prohibited from 90 days before screening until the end of treatment: any GLP-1 receptor agonist
(for example semaglutide, liraglutide, dulaglutide, tirzepatide); and any drug whose physiologic effect is appetite
suppression (for example phentermine). A prohibited medication started during treatment is a major protocol
deviation.

## 9 Assessments

### 9.1 Week 24 body weight

The Week 24 body weight used for the primary endpoint is the observation within ±7 days of the Week 24 target date,
as defined in SAP 9.4.1. A visit labeled WEEK 24 that falls outside that window does not qualify; an unscheduled
visit inside the window does.
