# Memo: proposed Amendment 3 to CSL-A (DRAFT, not approved)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. DRAFT. Not approved and not implemented in any SAP version. Nothing in this memo changes how
endpoints are calculated.

## 1 Proposal

The study team proposes widening the Week 24 analysis window further, to ±21 days around the target date, to
recover participants whose Week 24 visit was more than two weeks late.

## 2 Status

This proposal is under discussion. It has not been reviewed or approved, and SAP version 2.0 (±14 days) remains
in force.
