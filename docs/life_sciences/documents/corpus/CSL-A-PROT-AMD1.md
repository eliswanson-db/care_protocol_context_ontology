# CSL-A Protocol Amendment 1 (protocol version 2.0)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Illustrative only; not a real trial.

## 1 Summary of changes

| Section | Version 1.0 | Version 2.0 | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 / SAP 9.4.1 | Week 24 analysis window ±7 days around the target date | Week 24 analysis window ±14 days around the target date | Week 24 visits at a high-enrolling site were often scheduled 8 to 13 days late (identified during routine site monitoring in September 2025), leaving otherwise complete participants without an evaluable endpoint |
| 5.1 | Weight-related comorbidities as listed in version 1.0 | Obstructive sleep apnea added to the list | Screening logs showed candidates with BMI 27 to 30 and obstructive sleep apnea as their only comorbidity failing screening; obstructive sleep apnea is an established weight-related comorbidity |

No other endpoint rule changes. Baseline definition, target date, record selection (closest to target) and the
tie-breaker are unchanged.

## 2 Implementation

### 2.1 Statistical Analysis Plan

The change is implemented in SAP version 2.0, section 9.4.1. SAP version 1.0 remains the definition for analyses
under protocol version 1.0, so both versions can be compared.

### 2.2 Effective date

Protocol version 2.0 is effective 2025-10-27.

## 5 Study population

### 5.1 Inclusion criteria (as amended)

Criterion 3 now reads: weight-related comorbidities are hypertensive disorder, hyperlipidemia,
hypertriglyceridemia, type 2 diabetes mellitus, prediabetes, coronary artery disease, heart failure, stroke,
peripheral arterial disease, metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and obstructive
sleep apnea, including any more specific form of these conditions. Central sleep apnea is not added. The
exceptions in version 1.0 (pulmonary hypertension, pregnancy-induced hypertension, congenital coronary artery
malformations) still apply.

The amended criterion applies to participants screened on or after the effective date. Participants screened
earlier were assessed under the criterion in force at their screening date.
