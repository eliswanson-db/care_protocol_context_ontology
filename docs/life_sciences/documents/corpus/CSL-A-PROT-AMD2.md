# CSL-A Protocol Amendment 2 (protocol version 3.0)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Illustrative only; not a real trial.

## 1 Summary of changes

| Section | Version 2.0 | Version 3.0 |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Weight-related comorbidities as listed in version 1.0 | Obstructive sleep apnea added to the list |

The SAP is unchanged (version 2.0 still applies).

## 5 Study population

### 5.1 Inclusion criteria (as amended)

Criterion 3 now reads: weight-related comorbidities are hypertensive disorder, hyperlipidemia,
hypertriglyceridemia, type 2 diabetes mellitus, prediabetes, coronary artery disease, heart failure, stroke,
peripheral arterial disease, metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and obstructive
sleep apnea, including any more specific form of these conditions. Central sleep apnea is not added. The
exceptions in version 1.0 (pulmonary hypertension, pregnancy-induced hypertension, congenital coronary artery
malformations) still apply.

The amended criterion applies to participants screened on or after 2025-11-01. Participants screened earlier were
assessed under the criterion in force at their screening date.
