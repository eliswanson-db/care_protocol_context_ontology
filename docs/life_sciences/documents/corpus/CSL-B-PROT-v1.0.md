# CSL-B Clinical Study Protocol, version 1.0

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. CSL-101 is a fictional compound. Illustrative only; not a real trial.

## 1 Synopsis

CSL-B is a 52-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of once-weekly subcutaneous CSL-101 10 mg in
adults with type 2 diabetes and overweight or obesity. Participants are randomized 1:1 to CSL-101 10 mg once weekly
or placebo once weekly.

## 3 Objectives and endpoints

### 3.1 Key secondary endpoint

Percent change in body weight from baseline to Week 24. The derivation is defined in the CSL-B SAP, section 8.3.
It differs from the CSL-A definition (see the endpoint interpretation note).

## 5 Study population

### 5.1 Inclusion criteria

1. Age 18 to 80 years at screening.
2. Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus, including any more specific form.
3. BMI of 27 kg/m2 or more at screening.

### 5.2 Exclusion criteria

1. History of an exclusionary condition as listed for CSL-A (medullary thyroid carcinoma, multiple endocrine
   neoplasia type 2, pancreatitis, type 1 diabetes mellitus, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia), except that a
   history of diabetic retinopathy is not exclusionary in CSL-B.
2. Any condition that is a recorded contraindication for any member of the GLP-1 receptor agonist class. A
   history of allergy to an unrelated drug is not exclusionary.
3. Use of a prohibited medication within 90 days before screening (any GLP-1 receptor agonist, or any drug whose
   physiologic effect is appetite suppression).
