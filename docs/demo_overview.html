Why it matters

Quality is the margin now

  • Value-based care ties revenue to outcomes. Diabetes carries two triple-weighted Star measures: blood-sugar control and medication adherence.
  • Care slips between visits — an A1c above target with no recheck, therapy not intensified, a refill gap, a panel that just changed PCPs.
  • Protocols drift quietly and surface after the measurement year closes, when little time is left to close the gap.

Built to be trusted

Governed, cited, synthetic

  • Every answer cites its sources — the tables, documents and definitions it stands on, with lineage.
  • Row filters and column masks are defined once and follow the data into every surface, including scheduled briefs.
  • Analytics, not point-of-care. Comparisons are risk-adjusted, and clinicians review every recommendation.
